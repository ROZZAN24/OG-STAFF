        🎓 Security Laboratory Key Concepts & Viva Principles
      


      

        

          
What Digital Signatures Guarantee

          
    
            
  • Authenticity: Verifies the sender actually holds the private key matching the trusted public key.
    • 
            
  • Integrity: Ensures the payload has not been modified in transit by even 1 single character or bit.
    • 
            
  • Non-repudiation: The issuer cannot deny having signed the document, as only their private key can produce that signature.
    • 
          

        


        

          
The Cryptographic Avalanche Effect

          

            In SHA-256, changing even 1 bit of input flips approximately 50% of the output bits pseudorandomly. In our demo, changing "Assistant Professor" to "Professor" produces a completely unrecognizable 256-bit hash, preventing any possibility of a hash collision under modern computation.
